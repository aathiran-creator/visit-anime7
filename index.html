HISTORY OF ANIME

The history of Japanese animation, or anime, traces its roots to the early 20th century, with the earliest verifiable films produced in 1917 /////////// en.wikipedia.org . artsandculture.google.com Early creators experimented with various techniques like cutout animation, but the industry struggled significantly compared to Western productions and faced further setbacks from events like the 1923 Great Kantō Earthquake . nippon.com A pivotal turning point occurred in the 1960s with the work of Osamu Tezuka, often called the "God of Manga." His series Astro Boy (1963) established the format for weekly, half-hour televised anime, effectively launching the modern era of the medium en.wikipedia.org . artsandculture.google.com Throughout the 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s, anime diversified into numerous genres and styles, growing into a global cultural phenomenon through influential works like Akira, Dragon Ball Z, and Sailor Moon . en.wikipedia.org Today, anime is a massive, internationally recognized industry known for its storytelling depth and distinct artistic identity, spanning content for all age groups

Popular Anime Series

Some of the most popular anime series are: Naruto: A story about a young ninja who dreams of becoming the strongest leader of his village. - One Piece: An adventure about a pirate crew searching for the legendary treasure called One Piece. - Dragon Ball: A story about Goku and his journey to become stronger. - Demon Slayer: A story about a boy who fights demons to protect his family. - Attack on Titan: A fantasy action series about humans fighting against giant creatures.
